One clean workflow

Familiar for buyers. Trustworthy for sellers.

The experience stays inside the storefront while validation and quote creation happen where they belong: on trusted servers.

01

Collect products

Buyers add one or many products to a quote list that remains separate from the shopping cart.

02

Share requirements

A focused request flow captures quantities, contact details and buying context.

03

Validate safely

The extension re-reads catalog data and rejects forged names, SKUs and prices.

04

Create the draft

A structured quote lands in TackQuote for the seller to price, approve and send.

OpenCart · verified in detail

A real buyer flow, tested end to end.

OpenCart 4.1.0.4 was installed, configured and exercised against a real store. The extension uses storefront events instead of modifying theme files and fails closed until its secrets are configured.

✓ Real store✓ Forged price rejected✓ Throttle verified

Collect without touching the cart

“Add to Quote” gathers products from product pages and category tiles. Buyers can price a dozen items without changing shipping estimates or triggering abandoned-cart automations.

Ask only for what matters

A three-step panel confirms items and quantities, collects buyer details and shows a clear confirmation. Logged-in customer details are prefilled.

Re-price on the server

The browser submits product IDs and quantities only. The extension resolves name, model and price from the catalog and applies the store's visibility rules.

Land as a structured draft

TackQuote creates a draft in the store's currency and returns the reference the buyer sees, ready for a seller to price and send.

Separate secrets

Store-to-TackQuote uses an API key. TackQuote-to-store uses a distinct feed token. Neither can be reused in the other direction.

Bounded requests

Quote lists hold up to 50 products. A browser session can submit five requests per ten minutes before the throttle refuses more.
